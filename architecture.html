Solution architecture

AI reads and explains. Formulas do the money. People decide.

Six layers, from the screens families use down to the PNC systems that already hold the signal. Every AI output passes a consent check, a schema check and, where it matters, an advisor.

The stack

Read top to bottom: a request starts on a screen and only reaches data it is allowed to see.

Privacy boundary. Raw check-in text never leaves a member's vault. Only themes, gap scores and answers the member chose to share cross into the advisor view. The model reading a check-in runs inside that boundary and returns themes only.

One request, end to end

Dale opens the app on his phone and asks: “Can Dad sell without us?”

  1. Sign-in and scope. The gateway knows it's Dale, a child of the owner. His scope: his own vault, family-shared documents and the deal timeline.
  2. Consent filter. Other members' answers are removed before anything reaches a model. Only documents Alex has released to the children are in scope.
  3. Plan. The agent picks tools: docs.search("consent AND sale") and policy.check(legal_advice=false).
  4. Retrieve. Clause 7.2 of the 1998 agreement comes back with its page and a 93% extraction confidence.
  5. Draft. Claude or GPT writes a short answer in the required schema (below), citing the clause.
  6. Check. The schema is validated; the policy layer adds “this is a legal question” and points to the attorney's review.
  7. Render. The adaptive UI shows the answer with a document card and the list of tools used.
  8. Log. Model, version, tools, inputs and output go to the audit log. Alex can see that Dale asked, not the conversation.

The contract every model must meet

Because both models must return this structure, PNC can swap or compare them without touching the app.

From prototype to pilot

Today: prototype

Static web app on GitHub Pages. AI responses are simulated with the same schema and tool trace a live system would return. No real client data.

Pilot: 90 days

10 families and 5 Private Business Strategists. Signal model trained on historical exits. Live models behind the gateway, advisor-only at first, then family-facing.

Measures

Check-in completion above 70%. First family meeting before the letter of intent. Next-gen accounts opened per family within 12 months. Share of proceeds retained.

Open questions to validate

Whether business-account signals may be used for Private Bank outreach under PNC's data-sharing and consent policies. Which model performs best per task on a PNC evaluation set. How advisors want the workbench embedded in their current desktop.
