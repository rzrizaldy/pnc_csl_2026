Solution architecture

AI reads and explains. Formulas do the money. People decide.

Six layers, from the screens Margaret's family and Alex use down to the PNC systems that already hold the signal. Every AI output passes a consent check, a schema check and, where it matters, an advisor.

One request, end to end

Sam opens the app on his phone and asks: “When will I receive my share?”

  1. Sign-in and scope. The gateway knows it's Sam, a child of the owner. His scope: his own vault, the split options Margaret released to the family, and the deal timeline.
  2. Consent filter. Other members' answers and themes are removed before anything reaches a model. Ben's buyer equity is in scope only because it's part of the options the family was shown.
  3. Plan. The agent picks tools: deal.timeline() and calc.gift_options(child="sam").
  4. Calculate. The fixed calculator returns $4.0M with an equal split and $4.5M with equity counted. Closing is expected next fall.
  5. Draft. Claude or GPT writes a short answer in the required schema (below), using only the calculator's numbers.
  6. Check. The schema is validated, and every number in the answer must match the calculator's output. The policy layer adds that this isn't tax advice.
  7. Render. The adaptive UI shows the answer with a share card and the list of tools used.
  8. Log. Model, version, tools, inputs and output go to the audit log. Alex can see that Sam asked, not the conversation.

The contract every model must meet

Because both models must return this structure, PNC can swap or compare them without touching the app.

From prototype to pilot

Today: prototype

Static web app on GitHub Pages. AI responses are simulated with the same schema and tool trace a live system would return. No real client data.

Pilot: 90 days

10 families and 5 Private Business Strategists. Signal model trained on historical exits. Live models behind the gateway, advisor-only at first, then family-facing.

Measures

Check-in completion above 70%. First family meeting before the letter of intent. Next-gen accounts opened per family within 12 months. Share of proceeds retained.

Open questions to validate

Whether business-account signals may be used for Private Bank outreach under PNC's data-sharing and consent policies. Which model performs best per task on a PNC evaluation set. How advisors want the workbench embedded in their current desktop.
