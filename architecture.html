Today versus proposed production

Today · static demo

All fictional records are downloadable JavaScript. Browser storage is shared across demo personas. UI hiding demonstrates permissions but provides no security. Do not enter personal data.

Pilot · consented referral

Start with owner-requested referrals and explicit member participation. No transaction surveillance or trained sale-likelihood classifier. A future classifier needs permitted historical labels, negatives, temporal holdout and comparison with simple referral rules.

Production · enforce on server

Authenticate each member. Authorize every read before retrieval or model calls. Encrypt data; scope access by member and document. Isolate model processing and prevent vendor training as contracted. These controls are requirements, not implemented features.

One request, with clear ownership

  1. Invite: relationship manager records owner permission and purpose; Alex confirms client scope and conflict process.
  2. Draft: member writes privately. Proposed model suggests themes; member confirms or corrects them.
  3. Share: member approves exact text, recipient and purpose. No private themes or small-family aggregate scores cross the boundary.
  4. Prepare: model drafts a brief using authorized answers and reviewed documents only. Every claim includes source identifiers.
  5. Review: Alex approves the brief for preparation. Attorney confirms legal findings. Onward family disclosure needs separate permission.
  6. Withdraw: revoke future access and invalidate derived briefs and retrieval caches. Explain required restricted retention and that previously read information cannot be unlearned.
  7. Record: restricted audit records capture consent version, source IDs, model/prompt version, output, reviewer and action. Legal/compliance set retention and legal holds before pilot.

Use AI where language is the problem

Themes and document retrieval

Input: authorized text. Output: candidate themes or cited passages, not legal conclusions. Check: member confirmation and counsel review. Demo: keyword rules and sample findings.

Brief drafting

Input: shared text and reviewed findings. Output: sourced preparation draft. Check: advisor approval, invalidated after changed permissions. Demo: scripted brief driven by shared state.

Calculations and ordering

Input: explicit deal terms and selected weights. Output: reconciled proceeds and gifts. Check: finance tests and specialist review. No language model needed for arithmetic or fixed role ordering.

Compliance questions before a real pilot

Determine the applicable entity, data type, use and recipients with PNC counsel. Regulation P scope and nonaffiliated disclosure limits do not by themselves resolve commercial-data reuse or every affiliate scenario. No claim of legal clearance is made here.

Representation and conflicts

Define who the advisor represents, document conflicts and separate engagements, and offer independent counsel or facilitator. Do not use a vulnerable heir’s private concerns as sales targeting.

Advice and records

A disclaimer cannot neutralize legal conclusions. Flag excerpts for counsel. Do not auto-release findings. Keep access-restricted audit records under a reviewed retention policy.

Pilot gates

Security testing, consent-comprehension testing, model evaluations and counsel sign-off precede real client use. No production connections are delivered by this repository.
