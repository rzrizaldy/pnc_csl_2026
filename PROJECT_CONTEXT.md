## 2. The concept in one paragraph

**Family Table** is a PNC Private Bank concept. It helps a family plan the sale of its business together, and helps its PNC advisor see the whole family a year before closing.

- PNC already banks the operating company, so business-account activity can flag a likely sale 12 to 18 months ahead.
- The advisor invites the founder, and the founder invites the family.
- Each member checks in privately. AI turns their words into themes, and a fixed formula scores where the family disagrees.
- The advisor sees the gaps, not the words, and brings a facilitator when a gap is wide.
- Every child sees their own number under each split option.
- The plan, the documents and the specialists sit in one place. The heirs meet PNC before the money reaches them.

**One-line value:** turn a family's private, unspoken worries about a sale into consented, advisor-ready signals before the sale closes.

## 4. The story and its numbers (fictional and internally consistent; change only as a set)

**The sale:**
- Buyer: NorthPeak Logistics. Offer $48.0M.
- Less: earnout $8.0M (contingent, excluded), debt $3.5M, fees $1.5M, estimated tax $8.0M.
- **$27.0M at closing.**
- Margaret's buckets: live on $9.0M, give to the children $12.0M, invest and give back $6.0M.

**Timeline:** offer in hand in Oct, letter of intent in Jan, buyer review Feb to Jul, closing next fall. The family meeting is **Thursday, Nov 12, 2026**, with a Hawthorn IFS facilitator.

**Signal:** sale likelihood 81/100, up 11 this month. The reasons and their weights:

| Reason | Weight |
|---|---|
| Payments to an M&A adviser and a valuation firm | 0.34 |
| Audited-financials request | 0.26 |
| $2.5M credit line paid down 14 months early | 0.21 |

**Split formula:** w = 0 to 1 is the share of Ben's buyer equity that counts. Each child's target = (12 + 1.5w) / 3; Ben's cash = target − 1.5w.

| Split | Ben | Claire | Sam |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equal (w = 0) | $4.0M | $4.0M | $4.0M |
| Balanced (w = 0.5) | $3.5M | $4.25M | $4.25M |
| Equity counted (w = 1) | $3.0M | $4.5M | $4.5M |

Claire and Sam bank elsewhere, so **$8.0M to $9.0M reaches heirs who are not PNC clients**.

**Themes** (stances from −2 to +2, from 4 of 5 private check-ins). Gap = (max − min) / 4.

| Theme | Gap |
|---|---|
| Equal shares | **1.00** |
| Sell now | 0.75 |
| Cash soon | 0.50 |
| Keep the name | 0.50 |
| Margaret's next chapter | 0.25 |

A gap of 0.75 or more recommends a facilitator.

**Core insight:** Margaret told Alex "I think the kids are on the same page" and plans an equal split. The family's widest gap is fairness.

**Documents found by Document AI:**
- Margaret's 2012 will (high risk): leaves company shares equally. After the sale the shares become cash, and the will doesn't say whether Ben's equity counts.
- Key-person policy, 2015 (medium): pays the company and ends at closing.
- Buy-sell agreement, 2009 (medium): valuation fixed at 4×, while the offer implies about 7×.

**Contingency:** if Margaret can't continue before closing, Ben runs operations for 90 days and the family decides within 30.

**Cited statistics (only these):**

| Statistic | Source |
|---|---|
| 13% of adult children use their parents' financial advisor | Cerulli 2019, via InvestmentNews |
| 75% of owners "profoundly regretted" selling a year later | PNC, *Begin at the end* |
| 50% of exits are involuntary | PNC Business Succession Planning series |

- Pilot gates:
  - check-in completion ≥ 70%
  - first family meeting before the letter of intent

## 6. AI, architecture and privacy

**The rule:** AI reads, sorts and explains. Fixed formulas do the money. People make every decision.

**The six layers:**
1. Channels: family app, advisor workbench, nudges.
2. Experience: adaptive card order (**rules today**, never claim a ranking model), the "Sorted for you" explainer, the Ask assistant.
3. AI orchestration: one PNC model gateway in front of Claude and GPT, an agent runtime with tools, guardrails, a review queue.
4. Deterministic services: sale-likelihood model (gradient-boosted, a hypothesis to validate), proceeds and split calculator, gap formula, document OCR.
5. Data and trust: member vaults, household graph, consent ledger, audit log.
6. PNC systems: commercial banking, Private Bank, advisor CRM, Hawthorn IFS.

**Model contract:** both models return the same JSON schema: `answer`, `card`, `citations`, `numbers_from`, `advice_flags`, `needs_human`. Every number must match the calculator's output.

**Privacy model (verified in the prototype; don't weaken it):**
- Nothing is shared by default.
- Each member can opt out of the anonymous family summary.
- The summary appears only with at least 3 contributors (`MIN_GROUP`), and shows ranges with no per-person positions.
- Unshared answers show only "Answer N is private".
- All user text goes through `esc()`.
- Check-in *status* is visible to everyone; content is not.

**Compliance framing:**
- Phase 1 needs no data-sharing approval: the owner authorizes the referral, or the RM flags it.
- The account-activity signal is Phase 2, pending compliance review (GLBA, information barriers).
- Each adult member signs their own engagement letter. Joint meetings run through a Hawthorn facilitator.

**Secret sauce (three layers):**
1. A signal PNC already holds.
2. Private-by-default answers: honest data no competitor can buy or scrape.
3. One household graph: heirs meet PNC before the money moves.
