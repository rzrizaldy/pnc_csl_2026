Where the customer is connected

One family, one advisor, and the PNC teams behind them.

Family Table does not create new PNC teams. It gives the ones that already exist the whole family's view early. This page names each team as PNC names it, says where it sits, and shows when it comes in: Align, Allocate or Activate.

What we checked, and what changed

Each name was checked against PNC's own pages on October 27, 2026. Six matched as written. Four needed a correction.

In the draftWhat PNC saysChange
PNC AdvisoryPNC's site calls the group PNC Advisory Services. It covers Corporate Advisory, ESOP Solutions, M&A through Harris Williams and equity capital markets through Solebury Capital.Renamed, and the subsidiaries named.
Commercial Banking, $10M to $100MPNC's Commercial Banking page says $10M to $100M. Two other PNC pages still say $5M to $50M.Kept $10M to $100M, citing the Commercial Banking page.
Hawthorn: "facilitation"The Institute's page names assessments, coaching, workshops and legacy preservation. It does not use the word facilitation. Hawthorn is PNC's multi-family office for families with significant wealth; no threshold is published.Described as coaching and workshops. Whether a $48M family qualifies for Hawthorn is a question for PNC.
Banking Advisor: mortgage, securities-backed lendingPNC's page says credit and cash management. The specific products are not listed there.Kept to credit and cash management.
Investment Advisor, Fiduciary Advisor: AUM and AUA wordingBoth roles are confirmed. The discretionary and non-discretionary distinction is standard industry wording, not something PNC's page states.Said in plain words: PNC manages the money only if the client grants that authority.

Confirmed as written: Private Business Strategists, Relationship Strategist, Wealth Strategist, Fiduciary Advisor, Banking Advisor, Investment Advisor, Corporate Banking, and the Hawthorn Institute for Family Success as a name.
