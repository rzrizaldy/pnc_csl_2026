02 / 10
Discovery before claims

Test who people trust with an answer.

Two owners or heirs

Ask about the last difficult financial conversation. Who could see the answer? Show sharing controls; ask them to explain the recipient.

Two advisors

Reconstruct their last sale handoff. Time meeting preparation. Ask about representation, conflicts and a manual alternative.

Decision rule

Record quote, consent to cite, friction and resulting change. If sharing is misunderstood, simplify it. If members refuse the bank, offer separate counsel.

Proposed interviews, not completed research. Never fill this slide with invented quotes.

The family experience

Sloane decides what Alex receives.

Write privately

No answer or inferred theme appears in Alex’s view by default.

Share deliberately

Choose one answer for Alex. Preview exactly what he sees. A private conversation remains an alternative.

Change your mind

Withdraw future access. Editing requires sharing again. Prior reading and required audit retention cannot be undone.

The advisor workbench

Alex prepares from permission, not inference.

Start with a referral

Owner-authorized introduction. Confirm client scope, conflicts and separate representation before joint advice.

Review shared concerns

See only the answers members selected. Approve a preparation brief; changed text or permission invalidates approval.

Route decisions

Counsel confirms document findings. Family reviews scenarios. Account introductions require individual opt-in.

Interactive demo: prototype/#/advisor/align. No anonymous small-family scores or leadership counts.

06 / 10
Specific AI, bounded authority

Language models prepare material for review.

Extract and retrieve

Authorized text → candidate themes and cited passages. Member confirms themes; counsel interprets documents.

Draft a brief

Shared answers + reviewed sources → sourced preparation draft. Advisor checks accuracy and permission before use.

Keep rules deterministic

Calculators reconcile money. Fixed rules order screens. Today’s assistant is scripted; no model or bank integration is live.

Production gates: server authorization, permissioned evaluations, source grounding, restricted audit and retention policy.
07 / 10
Why PNC could win

Make a reliable family handoff part of the relationship.

Access

An existing commercial relationship may support a timely referral. It is neither exclusive nor automatic permission to reuse data.

Execution

Coordinate relationship managers, wealth advisors and family specialists with explicit permissions and named review owners.

Defensibility to earn

Competitors can copy the interface. Demonstrated trust, adoption and better outcomes would build an advantage over time.

Positioning hypothesis. No claim that PNC uniquely sees sales or owns private family concerns.
08 / 10
Trust and the business incentive

Participation never requires becoming a PNC client.

Know the client

Define representation and conflicts. Offer independent counsel and separate conversations.

Limit disclosure

No private theme aggregation. No automatic legal findings. Revoke future access and invalidate derived briefs.

Separate service from sales

Members can choose any bank. Measure voluntary relationships; do not target heirs using private distress.

Design requirements, not legal clearance. CFPB Regulation P §§1016.1 and 1016.10 inform questions for counsel.
09 / 10
A bounded pilot and a clear decision

Ask for a 90-day feasibility pilot with ten opt-in families.

Trust and time

Five advisors. Target ≥80% sharing comprehension and ≥25% lower median preparation time versus baseline. Any unauthorized disclosure stops the pilot.

Cost assumption

$150k cap: $90k implementation, $40k review/testing, $20k operations. At 0.50% annual gross fees, $30M incremental fee-bearing AUM covers this before costs.

Business follow-up

At 12 months compare voluntary introductions and incremental assets with matched families. No internal-transfer double counting. Ten families cannot prove causal retention lift.

All pilot targets and costs are planning assumptions. Interviews, budget validation and counsel/security approval precede real client use.
