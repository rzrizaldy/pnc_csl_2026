The family experience reduces to three steps, the core of the journey. Align: each member checks in privately; natural-language processing extracts themes inside their own vault, and the member decides, answer by answer, what the advisor may read. Allocate: each child sees their outcome under every distribution option, votes privately, and a decision tree narrows twelve possible plans to one; every figure comes from a fixed formula. Activate: questions go to the advisor, not a chatbot; the AI drafts, the advisor reviews it before it is sent. Each tablet links to the same screen in the live prototype, so the audience can try it. This addresses Margaret’s friction directly: a guided experience rather than a dense dashboard, and no direct confrontation at the dinner table.
