Source: Family Table prototype, advisor view. Family data and figures are fictional; AI steps are simulated, formulas are real.

One workbench, four features, one per stage, each shown by the part of the screen that matters. Explore: the sale signal scores PNC’s own business-account activity, 81 out of 100, twelve to eighteen months before closing; the advisor decides when to engage the founder. Align: the family brief turns four of five private check-ins into themes with no names; the widest divergence is fairness, at 1.00, against Margaret’s belief that her children agree; the advisor approves the brief and brings in a Hawthorn facilitator above 0.75. Allocate: the meeting guide drafts a 90-minute agenda from the approved brief; the advisor leads the meeting. Activate: the inbox drafts each reply from the plan and the calculator, runs privacy and advice checks, and waits for the advisor to approve before it is sent. Governance holds throughout: nothing reaches the family until the advisor approves it.
