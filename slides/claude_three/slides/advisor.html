This is the advisor’s AI brief, the workbench at the Align stage, and it shows the advisor model in one screen. First, the proof point: four of five family members are now in the brief, up from one, the founder; the AI reads their private check-ins into themes, with no names. Second, the widest divergence is fairness, at 1.00, scored by a fixed formula rather than a model. Third, that tests the founder’s view: Margaret believes her children agree, and the family does not. Fourth, a gap above 0.75 brings in a Hawthorn facilitator, so human facilitation arrives before tension surfaces. Fifth, the advisor reads a member’s words only if that member chose to share them.
