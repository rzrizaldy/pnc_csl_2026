The advisor side, stage by stage: the signal in Explore, then one AI tool for each core stage, Align, Allocate and Activate. Each row says what the AI does and what Alex decides, because the rule is that Alex reviews every output before any client action. The signal comes from PNC’s own business-account data, a year before closing. The brief is the core insight: Margaret told Alex the kids are on the same page, but the family’s widest gap is fairness. Alex sees it as a range, with no names. The AI then turns the brief into a 90-minute meeting guide with screens to present, drafts replies to the family that Alex sends, and routes tasks to five specialist teams. It also flags the $8 to 9 million heading to two heirs who bank elsewhere. This answers Alex’s friction: hearing only the founder, undocumented concerns, and capabilities spread across business units.
