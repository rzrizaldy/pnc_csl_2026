Source: Family Table prototype, advisor view. Family data and figures are fictional; AI steps are simulated, formulas are real.

One workbench, four features, one per stage; the screen shows the family brief at the Align stage. Read the table across: what the AI prepares, and what the advisor decides. In Explore, the sale signal scores PNC’s own business-account activity and flags a likely sale twelve to eighteen months out; the advisor decides when to engage the founder. In Align, the family brief turns four of five private check-ins into themes with no names; the widest divergence is fairness, at 1.00, scored by a fixed formula; the advisor approves the brief and brings in a Hawthorn facilitator above 0.75. In Allocate, the meeting guide drafts a 90-minute agenda and presentation screens; the advisor leads the meeting. In Activate, the inbox drafts replies and routes work to five specialist teams; the advisor approves every reply. Governance holds throughout: nothing reaches the family until the advisor approves it.
