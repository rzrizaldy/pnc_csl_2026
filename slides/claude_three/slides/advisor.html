Source: Family Table prototype, advisor view. Family data and figures are fictional; AI steps are simulated, formulas are real.

The workbench starts with the sale signal: in Explore, the AI scores PNC’s own business-account activity, 81 out of 100, twelve to eighteen months before closing, and the advisor decides when to engage the founder. From there, the work runs through three pillars, the core of the journey. Align: the family brief turns four of five private check-ins into themes with no names; the widest divergence is fairness, at 1.00, against Margaret’s belief that her children agree; the advisor approves the brief and brings in a Hawthorn facilitator above 0.75. Allocate: the meeting guide drafts a 90-minute agenda from the approved brief; the advisor leads the meeting. Activate: the inbox drafts each reply from the plan and the calculator and waits for the advisor’s approval before it is sent. Governance is the foundation under all three pillars: nothing reaches the family until the advisor approves it, every output is logged, and the advisor sees divergence, never individual responses.
