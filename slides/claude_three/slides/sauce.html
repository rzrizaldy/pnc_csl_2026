Core innovation

A consented family-intelligence layer on PNC’s proprietary commercial signal. AI analyses, formulas calculate and the advisor decides, seeing divergence but never individual responses.

Wilson figures are fictional; revenue assumes an illustrative 0.8% blended fee, to validate with PNC. Signal use is subject to compliance review.

Generative AI and alerts are easy to replicate; these three assets are not, and each enables a stage of the journey. The proprietary early signal enables Explore: PNC banks the operating company, so its own account data can indicate a sale a year before closing, while external wealth managers see the assets only after they move. Private-by-default family intelligence enables Align and Allocate: natural-language processing converts each member’s private input into themes inside their own vault, a fixed formula measures divergence, and the advisor sees the size of a disagreement, never individual responses, and only when at least three members contribute. Candid, consented family data cannot be bought or scraped. The unified household graph enables Activate and Continue: the next generation becomes a PNC relationship before the wealth transfers. In the Wilson case, $27.0M is retained at close, client relationships grow from three to five, $8.5M is captured with the next generation, and annual revenue is about $216K at an illustrative 0.8% blended fee.
