Read the stack from the bottom up. The foundation is PNC’s own systems: commercial banking data flags a likely sale 12 to 18 months ahead, the first proprietary asset, and Business Banking, the Private Bank, wealth and Hawthorn IFS work here. Above it, data and consent: member vaults, a consent ledger and the household graph linking the business to owners and heirs, the second proprietary asset; each member controls sharing and compliance audits the ledger. Then deterministic formulas: the proceeds calculator, the gap score and the decision tree, owned by tax and trust specialists, so every number is auditable. The AI layer sits above: NLP themes, Document AI and drafted replies behind one model gateway, with a consent filter and a review queue; this private-by-default family intelligence is the third proprietary asset, and Alex reviews every output. On top, the experience: one plan, two views, for Margaret’s family and for Alex. In the Wilson case that stack keeps $27.0M at PNC at close, grows client relationships from three to five, captures $8.5M with the next generation, and earns about $216K a year at an illustrative 0.8% blended fee.
