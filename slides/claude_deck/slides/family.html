Their own number first

Sam sees his number under all three splits, then votes privately. The tally shows only at three votes, with no names.

Ask Alex

A question goes to Alex, not a chatbot. The reply comes with its numbers, and a call is one tap away.

AI: drafts the reply from the calculator; Alex edits and approves before it sends.

Private by default

Claire decides, answer by answer, what Alex can read. Her mother never sees it.

AI: a language model detects themes, including an unspoken concern.
