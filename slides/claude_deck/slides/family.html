Source: Family Table prototype (mobile). The Wilson family, portraits and figures are fictional; AI steps are simulated, formulas are real.

Five moments on the family side, all on a phone. Explore and Onboard are the lead-in: before Margaret ever talks to PNC, the preview shows what a sale could mean for a family like hers, and once she joins, PNC has already filled in the facts and she says what fair means to her. Then the core journey, where the family actually decides. Align: Claire checks in privately; natural-language processing tags themes inside her own vault, and she chooses what Alex may read. Allocate: each child sees their own number under every split, votes privately, and a decision tree narrows 12 possible plans to one. Activate: questions go to Alex, not a chatbot; the AI drafts, Alex checks and sends. This answers Margaret's friction: a guided portal, not a dense dashboard, and no direct confrontation at the dinner table.
