Solution architecture

One gateway, two models, and a person behind every decision

1 · Channels

Family app on any phone · Advisor workbench · Email and SMS nudges

2 · Experience

Screens ranked per person · Ask bar · “Sorted for you” explainer

3 · AI orchestration

Gateway to Claude or GPT · Agent with tools · Guardrails · Review queue

4 · Fixed services

Sale-likelihood model · Proceeds calculator · Gap score · Document OCR

5 · Data and trust

Member vaults · Household graph · Consent ledger · Audit log

6 · PNC systems

Commercial banking · Private Bank · Advisor CRM · Hawthorn IFS

Raw check-in text never leaves a member’s vault. Both models must return the same output schema, so PNC can swap them per task.

Read top to bottom. A request starts on a screen, passes a consent filter, and only reaches data that person may see. Models extract and explain; fixed services do every number; the review queue puts an advisor between any model output and the family. Full detail, including one request traced end to end, is on the project site’s architecture page.
