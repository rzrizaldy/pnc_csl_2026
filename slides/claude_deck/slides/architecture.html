Solution architecture

One gateway, two models, and a person behind every decision

1 · Channels

Family app on any phone · Advisor workbench · Email and SMS nudges

2 · Experience

Screens ranked per person · Ask bar · “Sorted for you” explainer

3 · AI orchestration

Gateway to Claude or GPT · Agent with tools · Guardrails · Review queue

4 · Fixed services

Sale-likelihood model · Proceeds calculator · Gap score · Document OCR

5 · Data and trust

Member vaults · Household graph · Consent ledger · Audit log

6 · PNC systems

Commercial banking · Private Bank · Advisor CRM · Hawthorn IFS

Privacy boundary. Raw check-in text never leaves a member’s vault. Only themes, gap scores and answers a member chose to share reach the advisor.

Model-agnostic. Claude and GPT must return the same output schema, so PNC can compare and swap them per task.

Source: Family Table solution architecture. Full detail and one request traced end to end on the project site.

Read top to bottom. A request starts on a screen, passes a consent filter, and only reaches data that person may see.
