Personas from the team deck. The Wilson family and all figures are fictional; needs drawn from PNC’s Business Succession Planning papers.

This is the spine of the product. Margaret's three goals and Alex's three goals come straight from our persona slide, and each one maps to a screen in the prototype. Margaret's friction is that her family avoids direct conversations and she won't use a dense dashboard. Alex's friction is hearing only the founder, and finding tension after decisions are made. The same system solves both: private check-ins give the family a safe voice, and the advisor sees the gaps early.
