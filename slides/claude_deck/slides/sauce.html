Generative AI and alerts are easy to copy; these three assets are not, and each powers a stage of the journey. The signal powers Explore: PNC banks the operating company, so its own account data can flag a sale a year before closing, while outside wealth managers see the money only after it moves. Private-by-default family intelligence powers Align and Allocate: natural-language processing turns each member's private words into themes inside their own vault, a fixed formula scores the gaps, and Alex sees the size of a disagreement, never the words, and only when at least three people take part. The household graph powers Activate: the next generation meets PNC before the money moves. In the Wilson case, $27.0M stays at PNC, relationships grow from three to five, $8.5M reaches the next generation, and fees are about $216K a year at an illustrative 0.8%.
