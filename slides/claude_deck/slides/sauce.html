The secret sauce & core innovation

Our edge is a signal only PNC holds and answers only a family member would give in private

1 · A signal PNC already holds

PNC banks the operating company, so it sees a sale coming a year before close.

AI: gradient-boosted sale-likelihood model

Human: advisor decides whether to call

2 · Private by default

Each member answers alone. The advisor sees the gap, never the words, unless shared.

AI: language model extracts stances; a fixed formula scores gaps

Human: each member decides what is shared

3 · One household graph

Business, owners, spouse, heirs, trusts and accounts in one consented map.

AI: none needed; built from PNC records and invites

Human: family approves; advisor routes specialists

Timing. Others see the money only after it moves.

Trust. Honest private answers can’t be bought or scraped.

One roof. Commercial, Private Bank and Hawthorn IFS.

Fixed formulas calculate every number; language models only extract and explain. Signal use needs validation against PNC data-sharing and consent policies.

Generative AI and alerts are easy to copy. Three things are not. First, timing: PNC banks the operating company, so payments to M&A advisors, audited-financials requests and early credit paydowns show a sale coming 12 to 18 months ahead. Second, private-by-default answers: family members tell the system what they will not say at the dinner table, and the advisor sees only the size of each disagreement. Third, the household graph links the business to every heir, so the next generation becomes a PNC relationship before assets move.
