AI advisor workbench

Alex hears about the sale a year early and sees where the family disagrees, not what they wrote

1 · Transition signals

Payments to M&A and valuation firms flag a likely sale 12–18 months before close.

AI: a gradient-boosted model scores account events and shows its top three reasons.

2 · Alignment brief

Gaps by theme, private answers sealed, and a drafted brief for the family meeting.

AI: a fixed formula scores each gap; a language model drafts; Alex approves every line.

Source: Family Table prototype. Scores and family data are fictional; the signal model is a concept to be validated on PNC data.
