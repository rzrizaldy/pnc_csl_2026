Discovery before claims

Test who people trust with an answer.

Two owners or heirs

Ask about the last difficult financial conversation. Who could see the answer? Show sharing controls; ask them to explain the recipient.

Two advisors

Reconstruct their last sale handoff. Time meeting preparation. Ask about representation, conflicts and a manual alternative.

Decision rule

Record quote, consent to cite, friction and resulting change. If sharing is misunderstood, simplify it. If members refuse the bank, offer separate counsel.

Proposed interviews, not completed research. Never fill this slide with invented quotes.
