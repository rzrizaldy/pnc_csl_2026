## How to record

### Recommended: Apple, recording the screen while the film plays (voice syncs automatically)
1. Open `family_table_rehearsal_v6.mp4` in **QuickTime Player**. The script is built into the video as subtitles: turn them on with **View → Subtitles → English (Script v6)**.
2. Plug in your best mic: AirPods, an external mic, or the MacBook mic about a hand's width away. Use a quiet room with no fan.
3. Press **Cmd + Shift + 5** → **Record Entire Screen** → **Options**. Set **Microphone** to your mic and turn **Show Mouse Clicks** off.
4. Click Record, then press **Play** in QuickTime (full screen is fine). Read each line as its subtitle appears.
5. When the film ends, stop the recording from the menu bar. The .mov lands on the Desktop.
6. Send me the .mov. I take the voice out of it, find where the film starts, and lay it on the 1080p v6 picture. You never have to line anything up by hand.

**If a line goes wrong:** keep going, then redo just that line at the end. Say "line 7 again" first and leave 2 seconds of silence. I'll splice it in.

### Alternative: Loom (same idea, slightly lower audio quality)
1. In Loom, choose **Screen Only** (no camera bubble, since the film already fills the frame) and pick your mic. **Full screen** is fine.
2. Start Loom, and after the 3-2-1, press Play on `family_table_rehearsal_v6.mp4` in QuickTime with subtitles on.
3. Read along, stop Loom at the end, then **Download** the MP4 (… → Download), or send the share link.
4. Send me the file. I take the audio out and sync it the same way.

Loom compresses audio and sometimes adds noise reduction that makes the voice sound "underwater", so Apple is better. Use Loom if you're on Windows or want the link-sharing workflow.

### Voice only (best audio, with the line-by-line option)
QuickTime → **File → New Audio Recording**, or Voice Memos on an iPhone held 20 cm away. Record each line with 2 seconds of silence between them. Send me the .m4a. I cut it on the silences and place each line at its window start above.
