# Family Table film v5: storyboard

A silent picture cut to a recording script (`../RECORDING_SCRIPT.md`, v5). Narration is recorded later and laid over it; there's no music.

**Brief (Rz, Oct 28):**
- Follow Adit's cut (`video/adit/family_table_silent2.mp4`) for the order and the family-and-advisor relationship.
- Every product shot sits inside a device: phone for the family, iPad for Alex.
- The layered stack (vantis.sh style) stays.
- More isometric schematics, so it feels like a product film rather than a screen recording.
- Only the numbers that matter: 55% and 5 of 5.

## Look

**Colours:**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Stage navy | `#0D1C44` |
| Slab navy | `#1D3270` |
| Line | `#3A5299` |
| Text | `#FFFFFF` |
| Soft text | `#C9D1E6` |
| PNC and advisor action | orange `#EF6A00` |
| AI | purple `#7C6CF0` |
| Family vaults and formulas | ice `#E8ECF6` |

- **Type:** Barlow 600/700 headlines, 500 for body, sentence case.
- **One world:** a true isometric projection (x → (cos30, sin30), y → (−cos30, sin30), z up), drawn as SVG frame by frame, on a faint iso grid.
- **Devices share that plane.** When a device is lying "on" the world it uses the same projection, `rotateX(54.74°) rotateZ(45°)`. It stands up to face the camera, then lies back down when it leaves. That's the move that joins every schematic to every screen; there are no hard cuts.
- **Layout of device scenes:**
  - headline top-left
  - a small live iso diagram under the headline that explains what the screen is doing, in sync with the taps
  - the device on the right
- **Motion:** cubic in-out, 0.6–1.2 s moves, one thing moving at a time. Dissolves are 0.6 s.
