## Tone
- **Calm and confident**, like briefing one senior person across a table. Short sentences, full stops at the ends.
- **Slow down on "55 percent"** (line 2) and **"all five"** (line 10). They're the only two numbers.
- **Three steps:** lift the three step names at the end of line 3, then land each one again where it starts a line: Allocate in line 8, Activate in line 9.
- **Land the ending:** a short beat after "Family Table.", then the last line slowly.

**Pronunciation:** Wilson **WIL-sun** · PNC **P-N-C** (letters) · AI **A-I** (letters)

## How to record (10 minutes)
1. **Room:** quiet, with soft furnishings and no fan.
2. **Mic:** sit about a hand's width from the laptop mic, or use earbuds with a mic.
3. **Rehearse once:** play `family_table_v5_silent.mp4` with `family_table_v5_cues.srt` loaded as subtitles. IINA, VLC and QuickTime with the file renamed to match all show it.
4. **Record, either way:**
   1. **To picture.** Play the film on one screen and record in QuickTime (File → New Audio Recording). Start the recording and the film together, and read each line as its subtitle appears.
   2. **Line by line.** Record each line on its own with 2 seconds of silence around it. Whoever edits drops each line at its start time above, in CapCut, iMovie or Premiere. A line that runs long can start up to 0.3 s early.
5. **Send** the .m4a/.mov to the team folder and tell Rz.
