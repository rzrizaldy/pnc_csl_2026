## Tone
- **Calm and confident**, like briefing one senior person across a table. Short sentences, full stops at the ends.
- **Slow down** on the numbers: "124 trillion", "55 percent", "499 billion", "ten families and three advisors".
- **Lift** the three step names in line 5, then land each one again as you start lines 6, 8 and 9.
- **Land the ending:** a short beat after "Family Table.", then "Crafting the family future, today" slowly.

**Pronunciation:** Wilson **WIL-sun** · PNC **P-N-C** (letters) · 2048 **twenty forty-eight** · 499 **four hundred ninety-nine**

## How to record (5 minutes)
1. **Room:** quiet, with soft furnishings and no fan. Light your face from the front with a window or lamp.
2. **Mic:** the film uses only your audio, so the camera is optional. Sit about a hand's width from the laptop mic, or use earbuds with a mic.
3. **App:** QuickTime Player → File → **New Audio Recording** (or New Movie Recording), quality Maximum. Voice Memos works too.
4. **Take:**
   1. Press record and wait 2 seconds.
   2. Read lines 1 to 13 with 2-second pauses.
   3. Wait 2 seconds, then stop.
   4. If you stumble, pause 2 seconds and re-read **that whole line**, then delete the bad take before sending (the splitter expects exactly 13 lines; see `video/src2/README.md` for `--cuts` if a take has extra lines).
5. **Send:** drop the .mov, .m4a or .mp4 in the team folder and tell Rz. `python3 video/src2/ingest.py <file>` re-times the film to it.
