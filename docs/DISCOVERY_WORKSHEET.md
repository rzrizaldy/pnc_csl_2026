# Primary discovery: four interviews, no invented evidence

Recruit two owners/adult heirs and two bankers/advisors. Interview separately for 15 minutes. Do not contact anyone automatically. Explain this is a student concept; ask permission before recording or quoting. Use role labels, not identifying client or financial details.

1. Tell me about the last difficult conversation about a business transition. Who was present? What happened next?
2. What did you need to know, and whom did you ask? What did you avoid saying?
3. Who would you trust with this answer: the founder's banker, your own advisor, counsel, nobody? Why?
4. Show Claire's unshared check-in screen. Ask “Who can see these words right now?” Then share and withdraw. Ask the same question. Do not coach.
5. Would a private call work better? What would make you decline the workspace?
6. For advisors: reconstruct the last handoff, preparation time, missing information and conflict checks. Do not request confidential client records.
